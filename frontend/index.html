Start the scanner to test every equidistant K1/K2/K3 Call and Put butterfly: buy K1 ask, sell 2× K2 bid, buy K3 ask, confirm only net credit after costs.

Long butterfly: BUY K1 at ask, SELL 2× K2 at bid, BUY K3 at ask. Strikes must be equidistant (K2 − K1 = K3 − K2). Every valid width is scanned — not only adjacent strikes. Calls and Puts are scanned separately. Exchanges, underlyings and expiries are never mixed inside one butterfly. Gross credit = 2 × K2 bid − K1 ask − K3 ask. Confirmed only if net credit > 0 after brokerage, taxes, exchange charges and configured slippage. Quantity must cover 1 × K1, 2 × K2 and 1 × K3. Each price is tagged (B) buy or (S) sell. During market hours only WebSocket bid/ask from the same snapshot can become 🟢 CONFIRMED ARBITRAGE. After hours LTP credit is 🟡 POTENTIAL and can never be confirmed. If net credit > 0, expiry payoff of the butterfly itself is never negative, so maximum expiry payoff loss is ₹0*. That does not remove execution, settlement, assignment or operational risk. Margin marked * is an estimate until Zerodha basket margin returns. Ranked by net minimum profit, then profit / margin, then max profit, then liquidity, then quote freshness.

Start the scanner to compare near vs far synthetic futures on NIFTY and F&O stocks.

For the same strike: synthetic future = K + CE − PE, with carry DF = erT when funding r ≠ 0. Current spread = Far synth − Near synth using executable bid/ask (buy ask / sell bid). Fair spread uses spot (or the near listed future) × e(r−q)T. Direction A buys the near synthetic and sells the far synthetic; B is the reverse — four option legs. Net calendar delta should be near 0; a material delta is a warning, not the trigger. Rank by grade, then |net edge|, then |mispricing|, then liquidity. HIGH-CONFIDENCE means scanner conditions passed. It is not a locked payoff like a box or a positive-credit butterfly. Historical percentile fills in after stored observations on this browser; it is never invented from one snapshot.
